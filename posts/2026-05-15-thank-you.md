A reader named Amit sent me this note about three years ago:

> dear francesco, i read your writings, but you have not written any
  new since 2019, so wanted to send you this note of acknowledgement

I tried to reply but there was no way to know this person better,
anyway the message was important to me.

Years later i am starting a new season for my blog, and i want to
begin from my thankfulness to this reader: thank you Amit, you gave me
the motivation to keep going. I hope to hear again from you some day.
