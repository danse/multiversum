This is the story of a slow drift, uprooting from a comfortable place
to nomading. I was documenting my experiences through a few years, and
this page connects them all. All posts can also be read
chronologically [here](drift-time.html).

## The beginning

Berlin was comfortable. I had a flat, a rhythm, a community. I wrote
the first post from there — a
[note](/drift/2013-07-19-note.html) about receiving positive feedback
from smart people, ending with a conviction: software should not be
secret. It did not feel like a beginning. It felt like a thought that
arrived and was recorded.

A year later i was still in Berlin, but something had shifted.
[Haskell had ruined me for JavaScript](/drift/2014-08-07-future.html).
I spent the year hunting for ways to bring Haskell's clarity to the
browser — and began to sense that the tools i wanted did not exist in
the places i was looking.

## 2015

The first pull away from Berlin. I
[returned to Catania](/drift/2015-04-08-back-to-my.html), chasing the
Internet's promise of freedom. The reality was harder than the ideal —
isolation where i expected liberation. I came back to Berlin, but the
flat no longer fit the same way. By October i was
[searching for Haskell work](/drift/2015-10-12-searching.html) across
every channel. Nobody answered. I resolved to build it myself. The
pulling away had begun, though i did not call it that yet.

## 2016

The year the movement became visible.

[On a train from Florence to Pisa](/drift/2016-01-25-rifredi-pisa.html)
i thought about digital nomadism and coworking in Catania — already
projecting myself elsewhere. A week later i was
[back in my Berlin flat](/drift/2016-01-31-a-picture-of-my-home-in-berlin.html)
after two months in Sicily, seeing it with new eyes. I called it "a
bubble of loneliness and freedom."

I told myself that [returning was not an escape](/drift/2016-03-05-not-a-getaway.html).
A short note on [adding clear fonts](/drift/2016-03-27-clarity.html)
to the site. Then back to Berlin, where i was
[torn](/drift/2016-05-12-windmills.html) between the city's momentum
and a pull toward Syracuse. I wondered if i was fighting giants or
windmills.

A [quiet moment](/drift/2016-05-13-a-quiet-hinterhof.html) in the
apartment — the closest i came to feeling at home. In August i attended
[Mangrove Education](/drift/2016-08-23-mangrove-education.html), a
conference on education and community. Then
[fading summer](/drift/2016-08-28-fading-summer.html): i drew a
balance after a year of freelancing, depleted but still writing.

September: a [long internal debate](/drift/2016-09-02-post.html)
about leaving Berlin. Then a
[coworking discovery](/drift/2016-09-08-post.html) — others shared my
struggles.

Winter: i was [trapped but adapting](/drift/2016-12-03-post.html),
adopting a nomadic mindset. The year closed with a note on
[video games as education](/drift/2016-12-10-post.html). Berlin was
still home, but i was no longer anchored there.

## 2017

The drift accelerated. Sixty-three posts — the most intense writing
year. The form itself loosened: from reflective essays to daily
fragments to something like a live feed of thought, as if the
destabilisation of place also destabilised the length of attention.

### January – March

The year opened [in the snow](/drift/2017-01-11-post.html), suspended
between excitement and uncertainty. I decided to
[quit Upwork](/drift/2017-01-16-post.html) and plan a new tool. By
March i was [fixing my freelance career](/drift/2017-03-10-post.html)
— my skills (solutions) did not find enough of clients' problems. I
[tried again with liquid work](/drift/2017-03-20-post.html), analysing
metrics.

Then came March 24 — four posts in one day. A new rhythm emerged, as
if writing shorter and more often could compensate for the loss of
stable ground. I wrote a [plan](/drift/2017-03-24-post-1.html),
a [success](/drift/2017-03-24-post-2.html), a
[constraint](/drift/2017-03-24-post-3.html), a
[conversation](/drift/2017-03-24-post-4.html). Three days later i
[abandoned the Mur project](/drift/2017-03-27-post-1.html),
[defended Haskell](/drift/2017-03-27-post-2.html),
[searched for work](/drift/2017-03-27-post-3.html), and cited
[Horace on missing the goal](/drift/2017-03-28-post-1.html). The month
ended with [tooling fights](/drift/2017-03-31-post-1.html).

### April – June

April: [Córdoba](/drift/2017-04-03-post-1.html). A different rhythm.
I felt [energised as a nomadic engineer](/drift/2017-04-04-post-1.html).
I planned the ["Crumbs" project](/drift/2017-04-10-post-1.html).
I [logged my whole day](/drift/2017-04-20-post-1.html), and it worked.
I [worked from home at night](/drift/2017-04-24-post-1.html), evoking
the teenage feeling of staying up late — the comfort of isolation.

May: [frustration](/drift/2017-05-22-post-1.html). Fewer hours, noise,
the gap between nomadic expectation and reality. I noticed that
[chat has its own tempo](/drift/2017-05-24-post-1.html), that
[coworking makes study hard](/drift/2017-05-26-post-1.html), that
[nomading made me a better communicator](/drift/2017-05-31-post-1.html).

June: [enterprise work is bad for meta-thinking](/drift/2017-06-12-enterprise.html).
[No-Googling](/drift/2017-06-12-laming.html) as hacking style. The
recognition that i had been [using money as a proxy for
value](/drift/2017-06-12-value.html). The tools i built and the tools
i needed were diverging.

### July – fragments

July was the most fragmented month — 21 posts, some only a sentence
long. As if the motion had reached a speed where only fragments could
be caught.

[Autonomous systems](/drift/2017-07-11-post-1.html).
[Ego and value](/drift/2017-07-13-post-1.html).
[A museum visit](/drift/2017-07-15-post-1.html).

Four posts on the 25th, three on the 26th, four on the 27th, four on

Everyone leaving their own itinerary. The drift became the subject as
much as the practice.

### August – December

[Walking the threshold](/drift/2017-08-01-post-1.html) — interrupted
by the environment. Then an [empty post](/drift/2017-08-01-post-2.html)
— title only, no body. The thought evaporated on arrival.

I decided to [be less aware of meta levels](/drift/2017-08-09-post-1.html).
I logged breakfast and [could not stop thinking about
work](/drift/2017-08-11-post-1.html). A [good day derailed by the
internet](/drift/2017-08-18-post-1.html).
[Time tracking looked like a disaster](/drift/2017-08-19-post-1.html).
I felt like [the weak link](/drift/2017-08-21-post-1.html).
The tracker [became the problem](/drift/2017-08-22-post-1.html).

Silence for two months. When i returned, i had
[created a #euronomads channel](/drift/2017-10-20-post-1.html).
A [crack in my story](/drift/2017-10-25-a-crack-in-my-story.html) —
the August stress left a mark.  
Maybe [someone was reading](/drift/2017-11-01-post-1.html). I planned
Disqus. I [entered Barcelona through a different
door](/drift/2017-11-10-post-1.html), attempting to integrate
opposites.

December: [Kasaputia](/drift/2017-12-15-kasaputia.html) — a bilingual
project about working openly in Ragusa. The current had carried me back
to Sicily.

## 2018

Sixteen posts. The writing changed — less frequent, more reflective,
more Italian. The drift was no longer accelerating. It was deepening.

[Giorgio, Claudia, and our way out](/drift/2018-01-05-post-1.html):
blogs as the best medium. ["Stacks" as a thinking
tool](/drift/2018-01-09-post-1.html). [Pride in Italian public
administration](/drift/2018-01-20-post-1.html) through free software.

[Different](/drift/2018-02-02-post-1.html): transformation,
fragmentation. [Leaving a house in Ragusa](/drift/2018-02-24-post-1.html)
(Italian). [The wave is rising](/drift/2018-02-24-post-2.html) (Italian)
— overcoming fear.

[Ibla or Catania](/drift/2018-03-01-post-1.html): solitude or people.
[Disappointed by the nomad community](/drift/2018-03-15-post-1.html).
[Over control](/drift/2018-04-21-post-1.html) — a short poem.

May was raw. [Fragmented diary](/drift/2018-05-08-post-1.html) —
capitalism, fear, struggle. [Domestic violence in
Catania](/drift/2018-05-09-post-1.html). [A bottom](/drift/2018-05-17-post-1.html)
— writing as lifeline. Then [back up](/drift/2018-05-18-post-1.html).

July: [Multiversum is already incomprehensible](/drift/2018-07-20-post-1.html)
(Italian) — the old posts felt dated. August:
[confronting prejudice](/drift/2018-08-09-post-1.html) (Italian) —
racism, sexism, a lost summer.

October: [thoughts about the next months](/drift/2018-10-03-post-1.html)
— scarce resources, considering Pisa, Berlin, Sicily. Berlin still
appeared as an option, but it no longer felt like a centre.

## 2019

Two posts. The drift slowed to stillness.

[Deleting remote work notes](/drift/2019-01-07-post-1.html): old
Upwork files gone, attitude changed.

[Watershed](/drift/2019-03-20-post-watershed.html): the last post. A
comprehensive research note — task management, Vue vs React, IPFS, the
multiversum concept. It reads like a starting point. It was the end.

---

## Afterword

There is no post after March 2019. The path did not reverse or arrive
— it simply stopped being recorded.

Reading these in order, i see someone trying to build a life around
three things that refused to align: the need to move, the need to make,
and the need to belong. Berlin was comfortable, but comfort was not
enough to hold me. The search for Haskell work, the experiments with
nomadism, the tools built and abandoned, the oscillation between
Italian and English, the long silences followed by bursts — all of it
circles the same question: how to live freely without dissolving into
isolation.

The blog did not end. It became a document of its own limits.
