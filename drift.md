Roughly between 2016 and 2018 i was documenting my personal and
professional life through blog posts. This page collects them all.

This is the story of a slow drift, uprooting from a comfortable place
to nomading, challenging my beliefs and lifestyle to the extreme. All
posts can also be read chronologically [here](drift-time.html).

## The beginning

I will not forget the day in 2013 when i wrote this
[note](/drift/2013-07-19-note.html): it was after presenting the first
Data Visualization meetup to BerlinJS. The presentation was a success
and i was moved by the potential.

A year later i was still in Berlin, but something had shifted.
[Haskell had ruined me for JavaScript](/drift/2014-08-07-future.html).
I spent the year hunting for ways to bring Haskell's clarity to the
browser — and began to sense that the tools i wanted did not exist in
the places i was looking.

## 2015

The first pull away from Berlin. I [returned to
Catania](/drift/2015-04-08-back-to-my.html), chasing the Internet's
promise of freedom. The reality was harder than the ideal — isolation
where i expected liberation. I came back to Berlin, but the flat no
longer fit the same way. By October i was [searching for Haskell
work](/drift/2015-10-12-searching.html) across every channel. Nobody
answered. I resolved to build it myself and created this blog. The
pulling away had begun, though i did not call it that yet.

## 2016

The year the movement became visible.

[On a train from Florence to Pisa](/drift/2016-01-25-rifredi-pisa.html)
i thought about digital nomadism and coworking in Catania — already
projecting myself elsewhere. A week later i was
[back in my Berlin flat](/drift/2016-01-31-a-picture-of-my-home-in-berlin.html)
after two months in Sicily, seeing it with new eyes. I called it "a
bubble of loneliness and freedom."

I told myself that [returning was not an
escape](/drift/2016-03-05-not-a-getaway.html).  A short note on
[adding clear fonts](/drift/2016-03-27-clarity.html) to the site. Then
back to Berlin, where i was [torn](/drift/2016-05-12-windmills.html)
between the city's momentum and the awareness that disruption was
unavoidable. I wondered if i was fighting giants or windmills.

A [quiet moment](/drift/2016-05-13-a-quiet-hinterhof.html) in the
apartment — the closest i came to feeling at home. In August i
attended [Mangrove
Education](/drift/2016-08-23-mangrove-education.html), a fun
conference on education and community. Then [fading
summer](/drift/2016-08-28-fading-summer.html): i drew a balance after
a year of freelancing, depleted but still writing.

In September a [long internal debate](/drift/2016-09-02-post.html)
about leaving Berlin. Then a [coworking
discovery](/drift/2016-09-08-post.html) — others shared my struggles.

In December i felt [held back but
adapting](/drift/2016-12-03-post.html), adopting a nomadic mindset. A
note on [video games as education](/drift/2016-12-10-post.html).

## 2017

The year i left. Sixty-three posts — the most intense writing
year. The form itself loosened: from reflective essays to daily
fragments to something like a live feed of thought, while the
travelling required me to be more efficient in my writings.

### January – March

The year opened [in the snow](/drift/2017-01-11-post.html), suspended
between excitement and uncertainty. I wanted to [quit
Upwork](/drift/2017-01-16-post.html) and started Abanico.

Those were my last peaceful moments in Berlin. By March i was in
Madrid, Spain, full of energy for the first nomadic experiences.

I went to a coworking and started rationalising: i wanted to [fix my
freelance career](/drift/2017-03-10-post.html) — my skills (solutions)
did not find enough of clients' problems. I [tried again with liquid
work](/drift/2017-03-20-post.html), analysing metrics.

Then came March 24 — four posts in one day. A new rhythm emerged,
writing shorter and more often before leaving the capital to travel
the inner regions of Spain. I wrote a
[plan](/drift/2017-03-24-post-1.html), a
[success](/drift/2017-03-24-post-2.html), a
[constraint](/drift/2017-03-24-post-3.html), a
[conversation](/drift/2017-03-24-post-4.html). Three days later i
[abandoned the Mur project](/drift/2017-03-27-post-1.html), [defended
Haskell](/drift/2017-03-27-post-2.html), [searched for
work](/drift/2017-03-27-post-3.html), and cited [Horace on missing the
goal](/drift/2017-03-28-post-1.html). The month ended with [tooling
fights](/drift/2017-03-31-post-1.html).

### April – June

In April i was in [Córdoba](/drift/2017-04-03-post-1.html), guest of
my friend Cristina. A different rhythm.  I felt [energised as a
nomadic engineer](/drift/2017-04-04-post-1.html).  I planned the
["Crumbs" project](/drift/2017-04-10-post-1.html).  I [logged my whole
day](/drift/2017-04-20-post-1.html), and it worked.  I [worked from
home at night](/drift/2017-04-24-post-1.html), evoking the teenage
feeling of staying up late — the comfort of isolation.

In May i was again in Madrid for a month; by the end of it i expressed
[frustration](/drift/2017-05-22-post-1.html) about being unable to
make my nomading sustainable.

Then a few scattered reflections: [chat has its own
tempo](/drift/2017-05-24-post-1.html), [coworking makes study
hard](/drift/2017-05-26-post-1.html), [nomading made me a better
communicator](/drift/2017-05-31-post-1.html).

Some other reflections from June in Sicily: [traditional workspaces
and hacking](/drift/2017-06-12-enterprise.html),
[no-Googling](/drift/2017-06-12-laming.html) as hacking style, the
recognition that i had been [using money as a proxy for
value](/drift/2017-06-12-value.html). The tools i built and the tools
i needed were diverging.

### July – fragments

In July i was back to Spain, and for writing it was the most
fragmented month — 21 posts, some only a sentence long. As if the
motion had reached a speed where only fragments could be caught.

The first notes are from Barcelona:

Everyone leaving their own itinerary. The drift became the subject as

### Summer and Autumn

I was still in Madrid collecting ideas: [walking the
threshold](/drift/2017-08-01-post-1.html), [not all those who wander
are lost](/drift/2017-08-01-post-2.html). Now i would like to [be less
aware of meta levels](/drift/2017-08-09-post-1.html), i find [i think
about work too much](/drift/2017-08-11-post-1.html).

But then i moved to a small town in Majorca, and working was a
struggle. [Connection problems undermined my basic
operativity](/drift/2017-08-18-post-1.html), [time tracking looked
like a disaster](/drift/2017-08-19-post-1.html). I felt like [the weak
link](/drift/2017-08-21-post-1.html). The tracker [became the
problem](/drift/2017-08-22-post-1.html).

My private life was destined to take that time, and it was a time of
growth. On my blog it left silence for two months.

I returned to blogging when i [created a #euronomads
channel](/drift/2017-10-20-post-1.html), at the time i thought it
would get far. A [crack in my
story](/drift/2017-10-25-a-crack-in-my-story.html) tells about how i
felt disrupted that Summer; in a positive way, but still a lot of
change to take in.

While i was travelling through Majorca, [some friends reached my
blog](/drift/2017-11-01-post-1.html). I will write with them in mind
from now on.

I [entered Barcelona through a different
door](/drift/2017-11-10-post-1.html), and while my writing was trying
to convey the disruption i experienced, i was desperately in love.

### Winter starts

After the magical Summer and Autumn, i came back to Sicily. It was an
humble experience in comparison with Spain, but i was full of positive
energy to make the best out of it.

In December i wrote [Kasaputia](/drift/2017-12-15-kasaputia.html) — a
bilingual project about working openly in Ragusa.

## 2018

Sixteen posts. The writing changed — less frequent, more reflective,
more Italian. The drift was no longer accelerating. It was deepening.

I wrote the first posts still in Ragusa: [Giorgio, Claudia, and our
way out](/drift/2018-01-05-post-1.html): blogs as the best
medium. ["Stacks" as a thinking
tool](/drift/2018-01-09-post-1.html). [Pride in Italian public
administration](/drift/2018-01-20-post-1.html) through free software.
[Different](/drift/2018-02-02-post-1.html): transformation,
fragmentation.

Then i started feeling like i should change again, move again:
[leaving a house in Ragusa](/drift/2018-02-24-post-1.html) (Italian),
[the wave is rising](/drift/2018-02-24-post-2.html) (Italian), [Ibla
or Catania](/drift/2018-03-01-post-1.html): solitude or people.
[Disappointed by the nomad community](/drift/2018-03-15-post-1.html).
[Over control](/drift/2018-04-21-post-1.html) — a short poem.

In the end i decided for Catania, but only a few raw notes are left
from that experience. [Fragmented
diary](/drift/2018-05-08-post-1.html) — capitalism, fear,
struggle. [Domestic violence in
Catania](/drift/2018-05-09-post-1.html). [A
bottom](/drift/2018-05-17-post-1.html) — writing as lifeline. Then
[back up](/drift/2018-05-18-post-1.html).

After Catania i started nomading again, but my routines were
completely disrupted.

July in Barcelona: [Multiversum is already
incomprehensible](/drift/2018-07-20-post-1.html) (Italian) — the old
posts felt dated.

August in Sicily: [confronting
prejudice](/drift/2018-08-09-post-1.html) (Italian) — racism, sexism,
a lost summer.

October in Berlin: [thoughts about the next
months](/drift/2018-10-03-post-1.html) — scarce resources, considering
Pisa, Berlin, Sicily.

## 2019

I decided to move to Barcelona, there my blog died after two posts.

[Deleting remote work notes](/drift/2019-01-07-post-1.html): old
Upwork files gone, attitude changed.

[Watershed](/drift/2019-03-20-post-watershed.html): A comprehensive
research note — task management, Vue vs React, IPFS, the multiversum
concept. It was like a core dump before giving up, the last post for
years to come.

---

## Afterword

There is no post after March 2019, as there was no way back from the
drift.

Reading these in order, i see someone trying to build a life around
values that refused to align. Berlin was home more than i could
realise when i left it.

The search for Haskell work, the experiments with nomadism, the tools
built and abandoned, the oscillation between Italian and English, the
long silences followed by bursts — all of it circles the same
question: how to live freely without dissolving into isolation.

The blog did not end. It became a document of its own limits.
